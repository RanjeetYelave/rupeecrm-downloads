Core Capabilities

Built for Everyday Business Needs

Everything required to bill customers, maintain inventory, issue purchase orders, and monitor business profitability from a single desktop app.

Fast GST Billing & Invoicing

Generate professional GST-compliant tax invoices and estimates. Automatic CGST, SGST, and IGST computations, item autocomplete, and multi-tab draft preservation.

Real-Time Inventory Control

Track physical stock balances, configure minimum reorder thresholds, update prices in bulk, and audit complete stock movement histories for every item.

Purchase Orders & Receiving

Issue supplier purchase orders, track incoming items, record partial shipments across deliveries, and automatically intake received stock into inventory.

Customer & Vendor Khata

Centralized party ledgers with credit balances, payment histories, receivables aging, and instant transaction statements for every customer and vendor.

Smart Restock & Forecasting

Understand which products are selling fastest and identify stockout risks in advance. See replenishment recommendations and draft supplier orders with one click.

100% Offline Data Ownership

No cloud lock-in. Your business records remain safely stored on your local computer with one-click automatic backups, local restores, and zero internet dependency.

Business Workflows

Designed Around How You Actually Do Business

From billing walk-in customers to reconciling supplier accounts and planning reorders, RupeeCRM organizes work into practical, time-saving workflows.

Sell Faster

Keep your billing counter moving swiftly with fast keyboard shortcuts, customer lookup, and automatic tax calculations.

  • Automatic CGST, SGST, and IGST computations with HSN code lookup.
  • Create quotations & estimates, then convert them to final tax invoices with 1 click.
  • Multi-tab billing: open multiple invoices and quotes simultaneously without losing unsaved drafts.
  • Split payment collection: Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, and Cheques.

Know Your Customers

Maintain centralized party profiles and keep track of every rupee owed to your business.

  • Customer directory with phone numbers, billing addresses, and GSTIN records.
  • Customer 360 view with complete invoice history, payments, and credit notes.
  • Instant Khata statements showing date-wise debits, credits, and net balances.
  • Receivables aging to spot overdue customer payments before they affect cash flow.

Stay in Control of Stock

Know exact stock counts on hand across all categories, with automatic deduction on sale.

  • Product catalog master with categories, cost prices, selling prices, and units.
  • Real-time stock balance tracking with configurable minimum threshold alerts.
  • Bulk product editor to update prices, tax rates, or stock levels across items in seconds.
  • Stock movement ledger recording an immutable audit trail for every intake, sale, and return.

Purchase Smarter

Manage supplier relationships, order tracking, and delivery receipts without missing items.

  • Vendor directory with payment terms and accounts payable tracking.
  • Create formal Purchase Orders and track order status from draft to fulfillment.
  • Partial goods receiving: receive items across multiple shipments with remaining quantity tracking.
  • Automatic inventory intake as goods are verified and received.

Plan Ahead with Smart Inventory

Avoid losing sales due to stockouts by anticipating what needs replenishment.

  • Interactive stockout risk breakdown pills: Out of Stock, Critical, Low, and Healthy.
  • Sales velocity analysis across 30, 90, 180, and 365-day demand windows.
  • Smart replenishment queue showing projected days of stock left and recommended reorder quantities.
  • One-click purchase order drafting directly from restock recommendations.

Understand Your Business

Get clear insight into sales trends, daily expenses, and overall business health.

  • Categorized expense logging: rent, utilities, transport, and daily business expenses.
  • Summaries of total sales, total purchases, and net customer outstanding balances.
  • Daily cash and bank collection registers for quick reconciliation.
  • Exportable financial statements for your tax consultant or CA.

Manage Your Team & Work

Keep daily store tasks and staff attendance organized in the same desktop application.

  • Staff directory with contact details, designation, and salary structures.
  • Daily employee attendance tracking and basic payroll calculations.
  • Task planner with Kanban board, calendar reminders, and operational notes.
  • OmniSearch (⌘K): quickly find any invoice, customer, or product, or run business math in seconds.

Run Multiple Businesses

Manage retail and wholesale entities or branch offices from one installation without mixed records.

  • Switch between different firms or business entities instantly from the top header.
  • Strict data separation: each firm has its own customers, catalog, GSTIN, and numbering.
  • Invoice-level billing firm selection with automatic customer safety reset.
  • Independent bank details, UPI QR codes, and custom print layouts per firm.

Your Data Stays With You

Keep your sensitive trade secrets, customer contacts, and margins on your computer alone.

  • Zero mandatory cloud storage: your financial records stay completely on your hard drive.
  • Automated background database backups created on shutdown and startup.
  • One-click local backup export and safe restoration whenever you upgrade PCs.
  • Smooth offline operation even when internet connectivity is down.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Getting Started in 2 Minutes

Follow the simple guide below for your operating system. No database setup or server installation required.

1

Download Installer

Click the Download Windows Installer button to directly download RupeeCRM-Setup-1.0.400-x64.exe.

2

Run Setup Package

Double-click the downloaded setup file. If Windows User Account Control prompts you, click "Yes" to proceed.

3

Complete Setup

Follow the standard on-screen wizard. Setup automatically creates shortcuts on your desktop and Start Menu.

4

Launch & Bill

Open RupeeCRM from your desktop shortcut, enter your firm name and GSTIN, and begin billing immediately.

Windows SmartScreen Note: Because RupeeCRM is an independently authored application distributed directly on GitHub without an expensive commercial EV certificate, Windows Defender SmartScreen may display a standard prompt. Simply click "More info" and select "Run anyway".
1

Download Disk Image

Click the Download macOS Installer button to download RupeeCRM-1.0.400-arm64.dmg.

2

Open DMG File

Double-click the downloaded .dmg file in your Downloads folder to mount the installer window.

3

Drag to Applications

Drag the RupeeCRM.app icon into your macOS Applications folder shortcut.

4

Launch Application

Open RupeeCRM from Launchpad or Applications. On first launch, your local business database initializes in seconds.

macOS Gatekeeper First-Launch Note: When opening RupeeCRM for the first time, if macOS displays a warning about an unidentified developer, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click "Open Anyway".
Specifications

System Requirements

RupeeCRM is engineered to be lightweight, responsive, and fully self-contained on everyday business computers.

Windows Requirements

  • Operating System Windows 10, 11 (64-bit)
  • Processor 1.6 GHz Intel / AMD Dual Core or higher
  • Memory (RAM) 4 GB minimum (8 GB recommended)
  • Available Disk Space 1 GB free space
  • Runtime Requirements Self-contained (Bundled inside installer)

macOS Requirements

  • Operating System macOS 12.0 Monterey or higher
  • Processor Apple Silicon M1 / M2 / M3 / M4 (or Rosetta 2)
  • Memory (RAM) 4 GB minimum (8 GB recommended)
  • Available Disk Space 1.2 GB free space
  • Display Resolution 1280 x 800 minimum
